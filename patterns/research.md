# Research Pattern

1. Define the decision or question the research must support.
2. Identify authoritative and secondary sources.
3. Define freshness requirements.
4. Collect evidence and record source/date.
5. Separate facts, assumptions, interpretation, and forecasts.
6. Resolve material contradictions where possible.
7. State uncertainty and missing evidence.
8. Produce the requested artifact.
9. Review claims against sources and acceptance criteria.
10. Persist conclusions and material decisions, not unnecessary raw context.
