# Infrastructure Assessment Pattern

1. Define systems, sites, and scope.
2. Inventory observed configuration and versions.
3. Mark evidence date and source.
4. Separate current verified state from historical findings.
5. Identify availability, capacity, security, supportability, backup/DR, and operational risks as applicable.
6. Distinguish requirement violations from best-practice improvements.
7. Rank remediation by consequence, likelihood, dependency, and effort.
8. Verify completed remediation before changing state.
9. Preserve unresolved risks and blockers.
10. Maintain a resumable action list.
